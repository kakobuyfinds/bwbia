03 / From discovery to decision

How to use a Kakobuy spreadsheet.

The value of a product list is in the details it helps you compare. A striking picture can start your search; clear measurements and current listing information should guide your decision.

Keep your shortlist simple: product link, selected variant, measurements, material, item price and questions still to resolve.

  1. 01

    Narrow the category first

    Separate shoe finds from T-shirts, pants, shorts and matching sets. Compare products that serve the same purpose before weighing small differences in appearance.

  2. 02

    Verify the source product link

    Check that the listing opens and still shows the intended item. Confirm the seller, color, size and variant. A saved spreadsheet entry can outlast a listing or its stock.

  3. 03

    Read beyond the headline photo

    Look for material descriptions, measurements and multiple views. Product photos can help compare appearance, but cannot independently prove composition, quality or authenticity.

  4. 04

    Check purchasing support and costs

    Confirm your chosen service currently supports the product and source link. Review fees, delivery estimates and available return terms before committing to an order.

The cost note

The item price is one part of the budget.

When comparing spreadsheet product prices, keep international shipping estimates and shopping agent costs separate until you have a current quote.

Estimated total = items + domestic delivery + service fees + international delivery + applicable duties and taxes

Actual charges depend on destination, package measurements, service terms and the order itself. Do not treat an old spreadsheet estimate as a final checkout total.

The quality note

A useful shortlist records uncertainty.

If a listing lacks a size chart, clear material information or photographs of the selected variant, mark that gap before comparing it with other products.

For jewelry finds, ask about material composition and care. For clothing, compare measurements with a garment you own. For seller comparisons, evaluate the evidence provided rather than relying on a “best finds” label.

05 / Straight answers

Before you follow a product link.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet shopping, sizing and what this page provides.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet generally refers to a curated table of product links intended for people researching purchases through Kakobuy. Its usefulness depends on the quality and freshness of its entries; a spreadsheet does not itself verify sellers or products.

Is this an official Kakobuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

Neither. This is an independent shopping guide with links to CAFANS categories. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet, operate Kakobuy or represent the brands mentioned.

How do I use Kakobuy product links?

Open the source listing, confirm the exact item and variant, and check whether your chosen shopping service currently supports that link and product. Verify the displayed details before paying. Links on this page open CAFANS rather than an agent checkout.

How should I compare shoe and clothing sizes?

Use the individual listing’s measurements and measurement method. For clothing, compare garment dimensions with an item you already own. For shoes, follow the seller’s foot-length guidance rather than assuming size labels match across brands.

Does a spreadsheet price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the product cost, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable duties or taxes separately. Final costs depend on the order, destination and current service terms.

Do brand names and product pictures prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, logo or picture does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or current availability. The illustrations on this page are editorial category artwork, not photographs of verified merchandise.
